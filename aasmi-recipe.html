Delicious Nepali Momo 🥟

By: Aasmi Joshi

Ingredients:

  • Flour – 2 cups
  • Ground chicken – 1 lb
  • Onion – 1 finely chopped
  • Ginger-garlic paste – 1 tbsp
  • Salt, pepper, soy sauce, momo masala– to taste

Instructions:

  1. Make the dough and rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Mix the filling ingredients well.
  3. Roll wrappers, fill, and seal the momo.
  4. Steam for 15-20 minutes until cooked.
  5. Serve hot with spicy tomoato chutney/achar !

Cooking time: 45 minutes

Serves: 4 people
