Fluffy Pancakes 🥞

By: Aavash Adhikari

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter or oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)

Instructions:

  1. In a bowl, mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. In another bowl, whisk milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla together.
  3. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. Don’t overmix!
  4. Heat a nonstick pan on medium heat and lightly grease it.
  5. Pour 1/4 cup of batter for each pancake and cook until bubbles form on top.
  6. Flip and cook the other side until golden brown.
  7. Serve warm with butter, syrup, or your favorite toppings!

Cooking time: 15 minutes

Serves: 3–4 people
